— Our Story

A long table,
under the leaves.

Casa Mika began, quietly, as a private home. In early 2026, the founder — Nguyễn Thị Minh Khánh — turned a 1,400m² garden in the My Khe Beach area into the table she had wanted, for as long as she could remember, for her friends.

My Khe is Da Nang's finest stretch of sand — a corridor of four- and five-star hotels, the boulevard of Nguyen Van Thoai, and the sea beyond. North of the main road, in Phuoc My ward, the garden was already here. The glasshouse was added for the rainy afternoons. The fish-tank wall came later, after a long evening spent watching one in another city. Everything else — the kitchen, the bar, the piano in the main hall — was built around the same idea: a high-end, independent place where the hour does not matter, only that you are still here.

Casa Mika is a small folding of her name. Minh Khánh, become Mi Ka in the easy mouth of friends. The house is, plainly, Mika's. The door is the same one that opened that first morning.

— What we believe

Four house rules,
quietly kept.

Not a manifesto. The handful of things we return to when a question — about a dish, a wine, an evening — comes up in the kitchen.

01 / Rhythm

Time, without hurry.

Service moves at the speed of the guest, not the kitchen. If you stay four hours, we will be quietly pleased. If you stay six, more so.

02 / Sourcing

The garden, the source.

Herbs from the courtyard, fish from the morning market, season ahead of menu. What is not in season is not on the plate, and that is not a problem.

03 / Kitchen

Two coasts, one table.

An Asian palate, a Western technique — and the reverse, when the ingredient asks for it. Never fusion as performance. Always in conversation.

04 / Hand

Quiet craft, by hand.

Hand-poured, hand-shaken, hand-plated. No machines on the bar, no shortcuts on the pass. The hour we save you, we spend on the dish.
